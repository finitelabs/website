Products and Capabilities

ESPHome Control4 Driver

Our ESPHome Control4 Driver gives Control4 a clean, reliable integration path for ESPHome-based devices.

It is a strong fit when a project needs more flexibility than standard device support allows:

  • Control ESPHome devices directly from your Control4 interface
  • Create complex automation scenarios involving ESPHome sensors and actuators
  • Monitor device status and receive notifications
  • Expand your smart home capabilities with affordable DIY components
Learn More

Home Connect for Apple Home Driver

Home Connect for Apple Home connects Control4 with Apple Home in a way that is straightforward to use and practical to maintain.

Key capabilities include:

  • Voice control of Control4 devices using Siri
  • Integration with Apple Home scenes and automations
  • Remote access to your Control4 system through the Apple Home app
  • Seamless experience for Apple users in Control4 environments
Learn More

MQTT Control4 Driver

Our MQTT Control4 Driver lets Control4 participate cleanly in MQTT-based automation systems, local services, and custom event flows.

Key capabilities include:

  • Local network operation without cloud dependencies
  • Compatibility with standard MQTT brokers
  • Authentication via username and password credentials
  • Self-reconnection capability following disconnections
Learn More
